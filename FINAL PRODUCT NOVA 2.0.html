PRIVACY & TERMS

Your app data is stored locally on this device using browser storage. This local version does not send your tracked data to a backend server.

Privacy Policy

This local version stores the information you enter (goals, food log, water, task history) locally in your browser's storage. Nothing you enter is transmitted to, or stored on, any backend server, and there is no account system or analytics collection in this version. Clearing your browser data or using the in-app reset will delete it. Because storage is local, we cannot guarantee its security if this device is shared or compromised.

Optional voice input in the Food assistant uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Depending on the browser, audio may be processed by the browser vendor's speech service outside NOVA. Typing works the same way and stays on your device.

Terms of Use

NOVA is provided for tracking general fitness and nutrition activity. It is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind, and nutrition/workout content is for informational purposes only — see the nutrition disclaimer in the app. Nutrition values are estimates and workout guidance is general wellness information.
